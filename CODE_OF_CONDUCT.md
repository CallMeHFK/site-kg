# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers commit to making participation in site-kg
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to a welcoming, respectful,
collaborative environment focused on the project.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being considerate and patient with other people
- Respecting differing viewpoints, ideas, and personal experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Using the project to crawl sites against their owners' terms or robots.txt intent

## Enforcement

Behavior that violates this Code of Conduct can be reported to the project
maintainer via GitHub. All reports will be handled confidentially. Maintainers who
do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith can be held accountable
by the remaining maintainers.

If you are unsure whether something is a violation, ask a maintainer — it is always
better to check than to assume.
